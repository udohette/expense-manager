EINTELIX EXPENSE TRACKER

Request account deletion

Eintelix Expense Tracker by Eintelix Innovations Limited lets you delete your financial records while keeping your account, or permanently delete your account and its associated data.

Delete data and keep your account

  1. Open Eintelix Expense Tracker and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose Delete all data, then confirm Delete data. Do not choose Delete account permanently if you want to keep your account.

Request data deletion without the app

Email etteinnovations@gmail.com from your account email address. Use the subject Eintelix Expense Tracker data deletion - keep account. Say whether you want all financial records deleted or describe the specific records you want removed, and state that your account should remain active.

Request data deletion; keep account

Data deleted and data kept

Deleting all app data removes your stored transactions, pasted bank-alert text attached to those transactions, notes, budgets, bills, savings goals, wallet records, debts, custom categories, and app preferences from this device and the cloud. Default categories and wallets may be recreated as empty records. Your authentication account, email address, user ID, and sign-in details are kept so you can continue using your account. For a request concerning specific records, identify the records you want removed; the rest remain.

We may ask you to verify ownership before handling an email request. No password or recovery link is required. If an email app does not open, copy the address into your email provider. See Local records and retention below for other devices and retained copies.

Permanently delete your account inside the app

  1. Open Eintelix Expense Tracker and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account permanently and confirm the permanent deletion.

Request account deletion without the app

Email etteinnovations@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Use the subject Eintelix Expense Tracker account deletion. Tell us that you want your account and associated data deleted.

Email a deletion request

If the button does not open an email app, copy the address and send the request using your email provider. We may need to verify account ownership before acting. Never include a password, authentication token, or recovery link.

What account deletion removes

Deletion removes the authentication account and associated synced records, including transactions, budgets, bills, savings goals, wallets, debts, categories, and settings. Data deletion alone is also available in the app if you want to keep your account.

Local records and retention

Deleting cloud records or the cloud account does not remotely erase files or cached records from other devices. Remove local records on those devices or uninstall the app. Provider backups and records required for legal or security purposes may be retained under provider retention policies and applicable obligations. Contact support for details about your request.
