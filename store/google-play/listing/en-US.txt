APP NAME
Eintelix Expense Tracker

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Track expenses, plan budgets, manage debts and build savings goals.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Bring your everyday finances into one clear view with Eintelix Expense Tracker.

RECORD INCOME AND EXPENSES
Add transactions, organise them by category, and find records using search and filters. Keep useful details such as payment methods, notes and tags with each entry.

PLAN YOUR BUDGET
Set weekly or monthly spending limits. See how much you have spent against each budget and spot categories that need attention.

BUILD SAVINGS GOALS
Create goals, set target amounts and dates, and record contributions as you make progress.

KEEP DEBTS ORGANISED
Track money you owe and money owed to you. Keep repayment details and due dates together with your records.

MANAGE WALLETS AND BILLS
Organise cash, bank, savings and business records in separate wallets. Track transfers and plan recurring bills in one place. Wallets record your finances; they do not hold or transfer real money.

TURN BANK ALERTS INTO RECORDS
Choose to paste bank-alert text and review the transactions created from it. The app does not automatically read your SMS inbox or connect to your bank account.

SYNC YOUR RECORDS
Sign in with email or Google to sync records across your devices when connected. Review your privacy choices, delete data or request account deletion from Settings.

Eintelix Expense Tracker is a record-keeping and budgeting tool. It does not provide loans, banking, investments or financial advice.
